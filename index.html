    Personal Information

    Let's start with the basics. This information identifies you and helps your executor contact the right people.

    About You

    Spouse / Partner

    Children & Dependents

    Add each child or dependent. Include adult children too — they may be beneficiaries.

    Key Contacts

    People who may help carry out your wishes or provide information.

    Financial Accounts

    List your bank accounts, retirement accounts, and investments. This helps your executor find and manage your assets.

    Real Estate & Vehicles

    List your property — homes, land, vehicles, boats, and anything else with significant value.

    Personal Property & Valuables

    List items with financial or sentimental value — jewelry, art, antiques, collectibles, family heirlooms, and more.

    Digital Assets

    List your online accounts and digital property so your family can access, close, or memorialize them.

    ⚠️
    Important: Do NOT type your actual passwords here. Instead, write a hint that only you or a trusted person would understand (for example: "the name of my first dog + my birthday"). Store real passwords in a password manager or a separate secure document.

    Debts & Loans

    List what you owe — mortgages, car loans, credit cards, personal loans. This helps ensure debts are properly handled.

    Insurance Policies

    List your insurance policies. Life insurance and other policies may be key parts of your estate plan.
